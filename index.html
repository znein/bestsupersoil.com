Questions people ask

What is super soil?

A living soil packed with nutrients and beneficial microbes that feed the roots over time. California Super Soil is fermented for 60 days, and the maker says it's around 800% more nutrient-dense than regular potting mix – so you water instead of mixing bottled nutrients.

Do I really never need to feed my plants?

That's the idea: the starter kit, AutoFlower kit and soil pack are all designed to take plants from seed to harvest with water only. Liquid plant food is there if you grow hydro or want extra.

How do I use the bagged soil?

Add 2–3 lb of super soil to the bottom of your pot, then fill the rest with regular soil. Premixed pots skip even that – they come ready to plant.

How fast does it ship?
